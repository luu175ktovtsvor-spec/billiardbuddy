## 6. 跳转填参验证

新增卡片复用现有 `handleCardClick` 逻辑：

```ts
params.set("tab", "workbench");
params.set("role", card.role);
params.set("customer", card.targetCustomerType);
params.set("packages", card.outputPackage.join(","));
params.set("intent", card.userIntentTemplate);
params.set("source", "role_workbench");
params.set("taskId", card.id);
router.push(`/dashboard/generate?${params.toString()}`);
```
